How to Use Sakicolor

Sakicolor is a color-selection tool designed to help you explore and collect colors from systematically generated color grids.

1. Overview

Sakicolor provides a large number of colors arranged in 9 × 9 color grids. Each grid contains 81 colors, with colors changing smoothly across the grid.

The grids are organized into three color families:

  1. Yellow–Red, gradually adding Blue
  2. Blue–Yellow, gradually adding Red
  3. Red–Blue, gradually adding Yellow

You can select any color from a grid and save it to your personal palette at the top of the page.

2. Your Color Palette

The palette contains six fixed slots. Empty slots are shown in light gray.

Adding a color

Click any color in a color grid. The selected color is placed into the first available empty slot.

Removing a color

Click a color already stored in the palette to remove it. The slot becomes empty again.

Other colors remain in their original slots.

Undoing an accidental removal

If you accidentally remove a color, you can quickly restore it.

  1. Click the empty slot where the color was removed.
  2. The previously removed color will be restored to that slot.

This quick undo is available for each palette slot independently. Only the most recently removed color from that slot can be restored.

3. Choosing a Color Grid

Below the palette are three groups of small buttons. Each button represents one 9 × 9 color grid.

The button itself displays a diagonal split between two colors. These colors indicate the two changing corner colors of the corresponding grid.

Click one of these buttons to display its color grid.

4. Understanding a Color Grid

Every color grid contains 81 colors, arranged as 9 rows × 9 columns.

The colors are generated by interpolating the four corner colors. Consequently, the grid provides a continuous visual transition between the colors specified at its corners.

5. The Three Color Families

Yellow–Red

This family begins with combinations of yellow and red and gradually introduces blue as you move through the available grids.

Blue–Yellow

This family begins with combinations of blue and yellow and gradually introduces red.

Red–Blue

This family begins with combinations of red and blue and gradually introduces yellow.

The three families are intended to provide different routes through the RGB color space rather than simply presenting a conventional hue wheel.

6. Creating a Composition with Your Palette

Once you have collected colors in your personal palette, you can use them to create your own color composition.

Start composing

  1. Build your personal palette using the color grids.
  2. Click Compose with my palette.
  3. Sakicolor opens the composition view using your six palette slots.

The composition view contains an 8 × 8 grid. Each cell can be painted with one of the colors in your palette.

Choose a color

Click one of the colors in your palette. The selected color becomes the current drawing color and is indicated as selected.

Paint the grid

Click a cell in the 8 × 8 grid to fill it with the selected color.

On a touch device, you can also drag across the grid to color multiple cells as you move.

You can freely replace the color of a cell at any time by selecting another palette color and clicking the cell again.

7. Saving Your Composition

When you are satisfied with your composition, click Save composition.

Your composition is saved in your browser so that you can return to it later on the same browser and device.

The saved composition is associated with your current Sakicolor palette. It is not uploaded to a server.

8. Editing Your Palette After Composing

If you want to change your palette after creating a composition, click Edit my palette.

Your current composition is saved before Sakicolor returns to the palette view. You can then add or remove colors from your six palette slots.

Click Compose with my palette again when you want to return to the composition view.

Your previously saved composition will be restored, allowing you to continue working on it.

9. Sharing Your Palette

Click Share my palette! to create a viewer URL containing your current palette.

The URL is copied to your clipboard. You can paste it into a browser, message, email, or other application to share the palette.

When someone opens the URL, Sakicolor displays the shared palette in viewer mode. The recipient can use those colors to create their own composition.

10. A Typical Workflow

A simple way to use Sakicolor is:

  1. Explore the color grids.
  2. Collect up to six colors in your personal palette.
  3. Click Compose with my palette.
  4. Select a palette color.
  5. Paint the 8 × 8 grid to create a composition.
  6. Click Save composition to keep your work.
  7. Use Edit my palette if you want to change your colors.
  8. Use Share my palette! if you want to share your palette with someone else.
