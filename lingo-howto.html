How to Use Sakilingo

Sakilingo is a language-practice tool for improving your speaking skills and maintaining your vocabulary. Choose a language, listen to a sentence, and try saying it yourself.

1. Choose a language

Use the language selector at the top of the screen. Sakilingo currently supports:

  • English
  • French
  • Japanese

A new set of practice sentences will be loaded when you change the language.

2. Read the sentence

Read the sentence silently in your mind, or read it out loud. Try to understand its meaning before listening to the pronunciation.

You can use the Previous and Next buttons to move through the sentences.

Click the translation button to reveal the translation. Try to understand the sentence without looking at the translation first though.

3. Listen to the pronunciation

If you are unsure how to pronounce the sentence, click the speaker button (found in the lower right corner) and listen carefully to the sentence.

4. Click the microphone button and speak

Click the microphone button and say the sentence. Your browser's speech-recognition system will convert your speech into text.

The recognized text will appear in the text box. Sakilingo then compares it with the target sentence.

Tip: Speak naturally and clearly. You don't need to speak extremely slowly.

5. See whether you were correct

If your spoken sentence matches the target sentence, Sakilingo displays:

Correct! 🎉

If it does not match, try again.

6. Practice again

Try saying the sentence several times until you can say it comfortably without looking at the text.

Then move to the next sentence and continue practicing.

About the microphone

  • Sakilingo uses your browser's speech-recognition capability. Your browser may ask for permission to use the microphone. Allow microphone access if you want to use the speaking practice feature.
  • If you don't want to use speech recognition, you can still use Sakilingo for reading and listening practice.
  • Depending on your browser and device, the device may keep microphone access active after use. To turn off the microphone, click the "Turn off Mic" button or close the browser tab.
