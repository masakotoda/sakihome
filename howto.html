How to Use Sakilingo

Sakilingo is a language-practice tool for improving your speaking and listening skills. Choose a language, listen to a sentence, and try saying it yourself.

1. Choose a language

① Select a language

Use the language selector at the top of the screen. Sakilingo currently supports:

  • English
  • French
  • Japanese

A new set of practice sentences will be loaded when you change the language.

2. Read the sentence

② Read the sentence aloud

The sentence to practice is displayed near the top of the screen. Read it carefully and try to remember its pronunciation.

You can use the Previous and Next buttons to move through the sentences.

3. Speak

③ Click the microphone button

Click the microphone button and say the sentence. Your browser's speech-recognition system will convert your speech into text.

The recognized text will appear in the text box. Sakilingo then compares it with the target sentence.

Tip: Speak naturally and clearly. You don't need to speak extremely slowly.

4. Check your answer

④ See whether you were correct

If your spoken sentence matches the target sentence, Sakilingo displays:

Correct! 🎉

If it does not match, try again.

5. See the Japanese translation

⑤ Click the translation button

When practicing English or French, click the translation button to reveal the Japanese translation.

Try to understand the sentence without looking at the translation first.

6. Listen to the pronunciation

⑥ Choose an accent and listen

Select the pronunciation/accent you want to use.

  • 🇺🇸 American English
  • 🇬🇧 British English
  • 🇦🇺 Australian English
  • 🇫🇷 French
  • 🇨🇦 Canadian French
  • 🇯🇵 Japanese

Then click the speaker button to hear the sentence.

7. Practice again

⑦ Repeat

Try saying the sentence several times until you can say it comfortably without looking at the text.

Then move to the next sentence and continue practicing.

Recommended practice method

  1. Read the sentence silently.
  2. Listen to the pronunciation.
  3. Hide the translation if possible.
  4. Click the microphone.
  5. Say the sentence.
  6. Check the result.
  7. Repeat until you can say it naturally.

About the microphone

Sakilingo uses your browser's speech-recognition capability. Your browser may ask for permission to use the microphone. Allow microphone access if you want to use the speaking practice feature.

If you don't want to use speech recognition, you can still use Sakilingo for reading and listening practice.
